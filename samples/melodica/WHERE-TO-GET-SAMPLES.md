# Melodica samples for fitting REED's body resonances

Drop the audio files (wav, flac, ogg or mp3) in this folder, `samples/melodica/`. This folder isn't committed: only
the numbers measured from the files go into the firmware, with credit.

Best for fitting: single notes held 2 s or more, no vibrato, no effects; a low, a middle and a high note if you can.

## 1. Wikimedia Commons (no account needed)

- "Rakes of Kildare", played on a Hohner Clavietta (an acoustic melodica), licence CC BY-SA:
  - page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Melodica_rakesofkildare.ogg
  - direct download: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/45/Melodica_rakesofkildare.ogg

  It's a tune, not held notes, but its longer notes can be measured.

## 2. Freesound (a free account is needed to download)

- Search: https://freesound.org/search/?q=melodica
- Narrower searches that tend to find held single notes:
  - `melodica note`
  - `melodica sustain`
  - `melodica C4` (or another note name)
  - `melodica single`
  - `pianica` and `melodion` (other names for the instrument)
- On each sound's page, check its licence (right-hand side). Take ones marked **CC0** or **Attribution (CC BY)**.
  Avoid **Attribution NonCommercial**.
- Note each file's page link, so the credit can name it.
