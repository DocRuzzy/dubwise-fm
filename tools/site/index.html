DUBWISE

A reggae and dub mixing desk you play live, for the M-VAVE FM-1. A SpIllt Beats release.

Play itin your browser: the same firmware code Cheat sheetevery control on one page Manualeverything, with pictures Installon your FM-1 Sound editorevery voice from your browser, over USB

In dub, the mixing desk is the instrument. Dubwise puts one under your fingers: mutes that land on the one, throws into a tape echo and a spring you can kick, King Tubby's stepped Big Knob, sirens, rewinds, and songs that keep every move. Nothing plays by itself: every drop is yours.
