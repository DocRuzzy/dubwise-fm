DUBWISE sound editor

Every voice of an FM-1 running Dubwise, from your browser over USB MIDI. Your edits go in as if turned on the desk: the song captures them and HOME + REC undoes them.

Needs a browser with WebMIDI (Chrome, Edge) and the FM-1 on USB.

Kits

A starting point for every voice at once, as SETUP's LOAD KIT does (HOME + REC on the FM-1 undoes it).

Your patches on the FM-1

Load one into the sound, or keep the sound in a slot (stop the FM-1 first: it won't write its memory while playing).

Files

A .dwpatch file is one channel's sound (48 bytes), the same as tools/dubwise_slots.py patch makes.
