# Dubwise FM: whole-project review (2026-11-08, after P0–P3)

Roadmap P0–P3 is built. P2.8 waits only on the fm1-base change proposed in `docs/upstream/`. The figures below come
from the device link (`./build.sh`) and the host profiler. CPU is estimated with the ×80 rule (the host is about 80×
faster than the device). That rule hasn't been checked on a real FM-1 yet. It's the largest unknown in this review.

## 1. Resources against the limits

**Flash:** the one-shots (32 KB) and the fonts (61 KB) are the largest constant data. The splash and screen saver are
drawn in code (1.5 KB).

## 3. Recommendation: headroom to keep

- **CPU: keep the average under ~60 % in Studio**, so a burst (a scene change, several notes at once) has ~15 % room
  before the guard's 75 % line. "Everything on" sits right at that line. That's acceptable, because the guard steps
  down by design, but don't add another always-on Studio effect without taking cost out elsewhere (§5).
- **Pool: keep ≥ 40 KB free** for the next feature buffer and fragmentation-free growth. There's 105 KB free now. §5
  can free about 40 KB more if a feature needs it.
- **RAM: keep ≥ 16 KB free** for stacks and the platform's growth. There's 40 KB free.
- **Flash: plenty** (377 KB). Samples are the only thing likely to use it up.
- **Before deciding anything further, measure on a device:** the PERF page with the "everything on" scene, and
  `plat_stack_least_ever`. If the device is faster than ×80 suggests (likely, see §5.6), there's room to raise
  quality. If it's slower, the reductions in §4 come first.

## 4. Quality: up, or down?

**Nothing needs reducing on present evidence.**

If the device measures worse than estimated, reduce in this order:
1. Turn off the cascade's second phaser on the mix (SINGLE costs half).
2. Make the voices half-rate (§5.1). This barely changes their sound.
3. Lower the Studio tier's threshold.

The dry path, timing and low end are never the place to save.

**Upgrades to consider once the device numbers are in:**
- **One-shots at full rate.** +32 KB pool and flash; brighter shots. Their character is fine at 22 kHz, so this is
  low priority.
- **A longer spin-back** (2.5 s, +20 KB). A longer drag back is more dramatic.
- **More moves per bar for motion** (48 instead of 32). Free if motion is compacted (§5.3).
- **Not recommended: a brighter echo line (22 kHz).** +37 KB, and the 11 kHz line's darkening is part of the tape
  sound the design wants.

## 5. Efficiency gains, now that the whole firmware is visible

| # | Change | Saves | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5.1 | **(Done 2026-11-09: bass, skank, organ; −7 % on the demo)** **Tonal voices at 22.05 kHz.** Bass, skank, organ and lead render at 44.1 kHz, though the memory plan (CLAUDE.md) puts tonal voices at 22.05 kHz. Run them at half rate with a 2× interpolator | ~6 % CPU with a full band (skank 6.5 → ~3.5, bass, lead) | Low; the kick and hats stay at full rate |
| 5.2 | **The second core** (`cpu2 = true`, `plat_cpu2_run`). Unused today. Give it the spring (or the echo and spring, ~12 %) while the main core runs the voices | 6–12 % of the main core | Medium; the design allows it ("optional acceleration") and needs a one-core fallback, which the guard tiers already are |
| 5.3 | **Motion in a shared event pool** instead of 32 fixed slots per bar. Most bars are empty | ~25 KB pool | Low |
| 5.4 | **Spin ring as µ-law** (8-bit) | 17.6 KB pool | Low; a rewind is lo-fi anyway |

| 5.6 | **Check the ×80 rule on a device.** First-order all-pass chains like the phaser's are latency-bound, so their true device cost may be far below the estimate | — | — |
| 5.7 | The kick's oscillator: a recursive sine instead of a sine call per sample | ~2 % | Low |

Already done during P3: the phaser's inner loop keeps its state in registers and has no per-sample divide.

## 6. Splash, shutdown and screen saver

What fm1-base allows:

- **Boot splash: yes.** The `APP_SPLASH` hook draws one still screen at boot, before the app starts. Dubwise now
  draws echo rings, DUBWISE and SPILLT BEATS in code, for 1.5 KB of flash.
- **Boot animation: only as the app's first frames** after start (the splash itself can't animate).
- **Shutdown screen: no.** The platform has no power-off hook, and the FM-1's power switch cuts power, so there's
  nothing to draw on. A soft-power hook would need hardware support that isn't documented.
- **Screen saver: app-level.** There's no backlight or sleep control in `plat.h`, so it's for looks, not power. The
  LCD doesn't burn in. Dubwise's saver is built: SETUP → SCREEN SAVER, OFF / 1 / 5 / 15 min, stopped only.

What images cost (240×240):

| Format | Size |
|---|---|
| RGB565 bitmap | 115 KB |
| 4-bit palette | 29 KB |
| 1–2-bit run-length logo (flat art like a label mark) | 2–6 KB |
| An animation | that times the frames, unless drawn in code |

**Recommendation:** keep drawing in code. If SpIllt Beats has a logo, send it: a 1–2-bit run-length version (a few
KB) can sit in the splash's centre.

## 8. Follow-up: capacity against a real song (done 2026-11-08)

The first version of this review budgeted CPU per dense moment and memory per buffer. It didn't size the song against
real use. Checked against real use:

- **Song length:** 192 bars was 10.7 minutes at 72 BPM, too short for a live set. It's now **512 bars** (28 minutes at
  72 BPM, 15 at 140). The song still fits its store object: 3,076 of 3,840 B.
- **Motion:** 32 moves a bar dropped moves on busy bars (one knob sweep across a bar is 16). It's now **one shared pool
  of 8,192 moves**: chunks of 8, chained per bar, up to 128 in any bar. Insertions and deletions stay bounded (a bar's
  128 moves, 16 hops). It's saved in 13 flash sectors at 0xB0000 ("MOT2"). Tested: 80 moves in one bar survive a save
  and reboot (`capacity.txt`).
- **Patterns** (64 steps a channel, 16 scenes with patterns) were already enough for a riddim and its versions.
- **Cost:** pool 238.7 → 259.0 KB of 344 KB (85 KB free):
  - motion +15.3 KB
  - song table +1.9 KB
  - the undo copy moved from RAM into the pool, 3.1 KB (RAM went down by 0.8 KB)

  This is more than the ~13 KB estimated: the estimate left out the undo copy and the chunk links. CPU is unchanged.
