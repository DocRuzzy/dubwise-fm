## 5.6 Dice: the sound randomiser

Fired only by the user, on the focused channel's voice: **ROLL** (a new patch inside that channel's and model's bounds table), **MUTATE** (a random offset from the current patch, ×AMOUNT 5–100 %), **HOLD** (marked fields are left alone), **HISTORY** (the last 8 results of the focused channel, scrolled with KNOB 4; any one kept by just leaving it there). Never touches levels, sends, the dub bus, patterns or other channels, so it can't jump the mix or destabilise a loop. Each roll is one undo step and is captured as motion like a knob (a pass replays it). Applied at once by default, or on the next quantise line (SETUP: DICE ON LINE). PRNG: xorshift32, seeded from the tick counter (tests set the seed: `param dice.seed`). Bounds per field per model are a const table reviewed by ear (the bass keeps a sine-like fundamental, the kick ends 40–60 Hz). Cost: image ~2 KB, pool ~0.6 KB (history), CPU ≈0 **Done (2026-11-09):** `app/dice.c`. DICE is the SOUND view's last bank (after the raw ones): KNOB 1 AMOUNT 5–100 %, KNOB 2 ROLL / MUTATE, KNOB 3 MORPH (says TO COME until 5.12), KNOB 4 HISTORY (8 a channel, kept per channel; 1.8 KB of pool, not the roadmap's 0.6: every channel keeps its own). ENV fires on the bank; HOME + EDIT fires anywhere; ENV held + a knob on MAIN or a raw bank holds that field (a macro: its targets), a lock drawn by its label. Bounds `DICE_B`: inside the raw ranges and narrower where the sound needs it (the kick's TUNE 0–85: 40–60 Hz; the bass's INDEX ≤ 90 and FEEDBACK ≤ 60; decays short of their ends); **to be reviewed by ear**. Never touches levels, sends, the bus, patterns, LIVE or other channels; the macros are left as they are (their dots show what moved). SETUP → DICE ON LINE (P_DICE_LINE 0x031) is stored in the settings record's spare byte (`rsv`, 0 in older saves: off), so no stored-format change. Seed: `param dice.seed` (P_DICE_SEED 0x032). Test `dice.txt`: a seeded roll's exact values inside the bounds, another seed differs, the first seed again repeats it, a hold kept through a fire, MUTATE 10 % within a tenth of each range, history played back, one undo step, HOME + EDIT from the DUB page, level / bus / other voices untouched, DICE ON LINE holding a field until the bar line (50 at +100 ms, 36 after it). ## 5.8 Drum step locks, flam, ratchet

Kick, rim and hats steps gain TUNE and DECAY locks (−/+ from the voice, 4 bits each) and a REPEAT field: off · FLAM (a ghost 20 ms early) · ×2 · ×3 · ×4 within the step. A drum step held: KNOB 2 is its TUNE lock (kick, rim; the hats keep closed / open), KNOB 1 its DECAY lock (KNOB 1 stays the pattern's length with no step held); ENV held + white cycles the step's REPEAT. Variety by hand, not by chance. Pattern format bumped with a converter (4.8's way). Cost: pattern RAM +1 B a drum step (3 ch × 4 patterns × 64 = 768 B, in the pool), CPU ≈0 (repeats are ordinary note-ons on the event list) **Done (2026-11-09, with 5.9; plan `docs/plan-5.8-5.9-steps.md`, approved):** no step grew. REPEAT is in a step's level byte (bits 4–6), and a drum step's TUNE and DECAY locks are its gate byte's two nibbles (−8..+7). "PST1" → "PST2" and song slots "DSL2" → "DSL3": the converter clears an older drum step's gate (a live-recorded length that would read as locks). The engine: REPEAT's sub-hits gathered with the step (FLAM a ghost `tempo/125` fine ticks, 20 ms, early; ×2–×4 evenly in the step), the locks handed to the voice for that hit (`voice_locks`: TUNE a semitone a step; DECAY the time × 2^(lock/4)). STEPS: hold-to-edit (an on step pressed turns off at its let-go only if no knob turned; before, holding a step to nudge it turned it off at once); KNOB 1 / KNOB 2 on a held drum step; KNOB 4 says GATE: NOT FOR A DRUM; ENV held + a white key cycles REPEAT; the big view draws them. Tests: `locks.txt` (the locks set, a locked hit higher and shorter: hf −53.4 vs −59.3 dB, rms −42.0 vs −39.7; FLAM two hits a bar, ×3 three; not on the bass; undo), `prob.txt`'s converter part (a PST1 save with live-recorded kick gates loads with them 0), `slot_transfer_test.py` (a DSL2 slot image loads converted); `session.txt` now expects a drum's GATE refused. ## 5.9 Step probability, seeded
