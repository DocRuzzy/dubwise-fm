Mutes, solos, throws (white keys)

White 1–8
Mute channel 1–8 (kick, snare, hats, bass, skank, organ, lead, vox). Tap toggles (on the next line); hold flips it only while held; tap twice: solo.
HOME+White 1–8
Solo, without the double tap. Solos add up.
White 9–16
Throw channel 1–8 into its echo / spring while held (harder = bigger). Double tap latches.
Hold White 1–8 + K1–K3
Level, echo send, spring send.
Hold White 9–16 + K1
Throw destination: ECHO, SPRING, BOTH.

Dub moves (black keys)

Black 1
Spring crash (with a throw held: adds the spring to it).
Black 2
Echo hold: freeze the loop.
Black 3
Dub push: feedback past endless.
Black 4
Tape dive: repeats bend down.
Black 5
Drop to wet: only the echo and spring.
Black 6 · 7
Siren A · siren B (held).
Black 8–10
One-shots BIM, HORN, LASER. HOME+: backwards.
Black 11
Drum and bass only (held).
HOME+PLAY
Rewind: spin back, come again from bar 1.
HOME+REC · GLO
Undo · redo (mutes, knobs, steps, scenes).

Every move above can be recorded into the song (REC).

Knobs and buttons (DUB page)

SELECT
The Big Knob: stepped high-pass, OFF · 70 Hz … 7.5 kHz (10, 20 or 40 steps: SETUP).
HOME+SELECT
Tempo. HOME+SEQ tapped: tap tempo.
Hold any button 1 s
Its help card; any press: back.
K1 · K2
Echo time · feedback.
K3 · K4
Echo return · spring return.
OCT− · OCT+
Quantise: beat, 1–32 bars, CYCLE.
PLAY
Start / stop (on release); again at once after a stop: tails off. Hold it while pressing a white key: that change now, not on the line.
ALGORITHM
Focus a channel; EDIT: its sound: MAIN (4 macros), then the raw banks (4 knobs each). A dot: a target turned since. There, PRESET: its patches; SAVE+PRESET: keep the sound (stopped). Last bank DICE: ENV rolls; ENV held + a knob: hold it; HOME+EDIT: roll anywhere.
PRESET
Next / previous scene, on the line.
SAVE
Save the project (lit: unsaved changes).
FX
Effects page; press again for the next bank.

STEPS (SEQ)

Black 1–8
Pick the channel.
White 1–16
Step on / off.
Hold Black 9 · 10 + white
Accent · ghost.
OCT− · OCT+
Page of 16 steps.
K1 · K2
Length (HOME: grid 1/8 … 1/16T … 1/32) · the held step's note.
K3 · K4

Patterns A–D and recording

HOME+White 1–4
Launch this channel's A–D on the line, from its top. White 9–12: every channel's.
SAVE+White 1–4
Copy the pattern into A–D.
HOME+OCT+ · OCT−
Double the pattern · clear it.
Mute key + PRESET
(DUB) That channel's next pattern.
REC playing
Record as you play. Empty pattern: from the one, a pass, then it loops. Else: overdub until REC.
REC stopped
Keyboard on; then PLAY counts in a bar and records.
Drums
Whites 1–4 are pads: kick, rim, closed hat, open hat.
SEQ
Leave the keyboard. SETUP: REC QUANTISE, MODE, OFFSET, CLICK.

ARRANGE: scenes and the song

White 1–16
Recall a scene (on the line).
SAVE+White
Store the desk in that scene (K4: with patterns).
REC
Song capture on / off: play, and every bar and move is kept.
HOME+REC
Undo (a capture, if it was the last thing). HOME+GLO: redo.
SAVE+REC
Clear the recorded moves.
Black 1–11
Section markers (INTRO … END). HOME+Black 1–4: VERSION, DUBPLATE, SPECIAL, RUB-A-DUB.
K1 · K2 · K3
Cursor · song length · OFF / ONCE / LOOP.

A first dub, in eight moves

  1. PLAY: the ONE DROP riddim starts.
  2. White 4 on the line: the bass drops out; again: back on the one.
  3. Hold White 10 on beat 3: the snare into the echo.
  4. Black 1: crash the spring.
  5. Turn SELECT up a step a beat: the mix evaporates; snap it back.
  6. Hold Black 2, then Black 3: freeze, then push the echo.
  7. Black 11: drum and bass; let go on the one.
  8. HOME, REC, PLAY: do it again and keep it as a song.
