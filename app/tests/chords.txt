# The skank and organ bubble (P1.6; D2, D3) follow the chord lane (default Am Dm Am Em, a bar each). ONE DROP
# carries them: the skank on 2 and 4 (steps 5 and 13: at 75 BPM, samples 35280 ..), the bubble low on the off-beat
# eighths (step 3, 7 ..) and high on the sixteenth after (step 4: the last by 850 ms, 26460; a sixteenth is 200 ms). Chords as root x 8 + quality: Am 72, Dm 16.
wait 600
tap PLAY
wait 850
expect hit6 26460
expect hit5 35280
expect chord_ch5 72
expect chord_ch6 72
# bar 2: Dm (the skank's first hit in it, step 5, at 4 s)
wait 3200

expect chord_ch6 16

wait 100
