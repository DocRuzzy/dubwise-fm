# The SMC-Mixer as the desk (roadmap 6.5; app/smc.c, after hortator-bt's): on the USB host, a device named SMC-...
# Faders the strips' levels (soft takeover), MUTE / SOLO on the line, REC a throw while held, SELECT the focus, knobs
# the Big Knob / echo / spring / sends / tempo, PLAY / STOP / REC; the LEDs and the levels fed back.
wait 600
listen
param usb.port 1
wait 100
usbh plug SMC-Mixer
wait 400
said SMC-MIXER THE DESK
expect usbh 2
# the LEDs on connecting: the focus (SELECT 1) lit, STOP lit, PLAY off; the levels sent (strip 1: 100)
expect_out USBH 90 18 7F
expect_out USBH 90 5D 7F
expect_out USBH 90 5E 00
expect_out USBH E0 64 64
# fader 1: far from the level (100) it does nothing; within 2 it picks it up, then follows
usbh midi E0 00 30
wait 30
expect ui.ch1.level 100
usbh midi E0 00 63
wait 30
expect ui.ch1.level 99
usbh midi E0 00 50
wait 30
expect ui.ch1.level 80
# a fader's run is one undo step (HOME + REC), and the mixer is sent the level back (its fader unpicked)
press HOME
wait 20
tap REC
wait 30
release HOME
wait 30
expect ui.ch1.level 100
expect_out USBH E0 64 64
# knobs: 3 ECHO FB, five clicks up (2 a click); 8 TEMPO, three clicks down (a tenth each)
param echo.fb 60
usbh midi B0 12 05
wait 30
expect ui.echo.fb 70
said ECHO FB 70
usbh midi B0 17 43
wait 30
expect ui.tempo 747
# MUTE 2 (stopped: at once), its LED; SOLO 3
usbh midi 90 11 7F
wait 30
expect ui.ch2.mute 1
said MUTE SNARE
usbh midi 90 11 00
wait 30
expect_out USBH 90 11 7F
usbh midi 90 0A 7F
wait 30
expect ui.ch3.solo 1
usbh midi 90 0A 00
usbh midi 90 0A 7F
wait 30
expect ui.ch3.solo 0
# REC 3: a throw while held, its LED
usbh midi 90 02 7F
wait 30
expect ui.ch3.throw 1
expect_out USBH 90 02 7F
usbh midi 90 02 00
wait 30
expect ui.ch3.throw 0
expect_out USBH 90 02 00
# SELECT 5: the focus, its LED (and 1's off)
usbh midi 90 1C 7F
wait 30
expect focus 5
expect_out USBH 90 1C 7F
expect_out USBH 90 18 00
# PLAY, STOP
usbh midi 90 5E 7F
wait 50
expect ui.play 1
expect_out USBH 90 5E 7F
usbh midi 90 5D 7F
wait 50
expect ui.play 0
# a keyboard that isn't an SMC still plays notes
usbh unplug
wait 200
