# The DUB page, P0.1: quantised mutes and solo from the white keys (docs/interface-spec.md 4.1). Test tones on
# every channel stand in for the voices. led_wN: 1 dim (muted), 2 bright (playing), 3 blinking (cued). Run by tests/run_tests.sh.
wait 600
listen

# test tones on every channel (SETUP's last item: setup.txt)
param dev.tones 1
wait 30
expect eng.dev.tones 1
# stopped: no clock, so a tap mutes at once; another tap unmutes
tapkey w0
wait 400

expect led_w1 1
expect led_w2 2
expect ch1_db -120
tapkey w0
wait 400
said UNMUTE KICK
expect eng.ch1.mute 0
expect led_w1 2
# playing at 75 BPM, quantise 1 bar: a tap is cued, its key blinks, and it lands on the bar
tap PLAY

expect t_playing 1
tapkey w1
wait 400

expect lands >1
