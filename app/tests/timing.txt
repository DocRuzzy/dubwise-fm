# The session's clock (roadmap 4.8): 240 fine ticks a beat. At 75.0 BPM a fine tick is exactly 147 samples, so each
# step lands on a known sample (hitN: channel N's last hit, samples since PLAY). A step's offset, a triplet grid,
# swing, a step played early (on the one at the very start; a tick early on later passes), a pattern launched on the
# line (from its top), one played early onto its launch line, and a held note's gate.
wait 300
# a step 20 ticks late: 4 x 60 + 20 = 260 ticks = 38,220 samples
clearpat 1 A
step 1 A 1 2
step 1 A 5 2 36 20
tap PLAY
wait 1000
expect hit1 38220
tap PLAY
wait 50
