# The sirens (P2.1; C9, D3) on channel 8: black 6 (A: two tones, a square LFO) and black 7 (B: a rising sweep),
# held to sound; the channel sends to the echo by default. Defaults: PITCH 537 Hz, DEPTH 7.5 semitones (the other
# tone 829 Hz), RATE 1.75 Hz.
wait 600
listen
wav siren-a.wav
key b5 down
wait 50
said SIREN A
expect ch8_db >-30
wait 2000
expect echo_db >-40
key b5 up
wait 300
expect ch8_db <-80
wavstop
wav siren-b.wav
key b6 down
wait 2000
key b6 up
wait 300
wavstop
# the SOUND view: channel 8's siren settings
turn ALGO -1
wait 30
expect focus 8
tap EDIT
wait 30
said SOUND VOX
turn K1 10
wait 30
expect ui.v8.pitch 80
