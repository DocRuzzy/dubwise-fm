# The MAIN macros (roadmap 5.3, spec 4.9): the SOUND view's first bank, four macros a channel. A macro's turn rewrites
# its raw targets along fixed curves (0: every target at its default); a raw turn afterwards leaves the macro where it
# was and MAIN shows a dot by it; the macro's turn is one step of undo with its targets. The ranges hold at the ends:
# no clipping, no DC, and the bass's fundamental stays on top at the index cap.
