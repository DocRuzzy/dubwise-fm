# Stop twice: tails off (roadmap 5.2, spec 4.1.3). A stop lets the echo and the spring ring out, as built; a second
# PLAY tap within 400 ms of it doesn't start the transport: the returns fade over 20 ms (no click), then the echo's
# tape and the spring are emptied ("TAILS OFF"). A start 400 ms or more after a stop works as before.
# Every channel muted, the snare's test tone thrown (a throw is pre-mute; a steady tone keeps the returns ringing
# steadily): the recordings hold only the returns, so a click from the cut would be the largest step in them.
wait 600
listen
param dev.tones 1
param echo.fb 80
param ch2.dest 2
param ch1.mute 1
param ch2.mute 1
param ch3.mute 1
param ch4.mute 1
param ch5.mute 1
param ch6.mute 1
param ch7.mute 1
param ch8.mute 1
tap PLAY
wait 1500
key w9 down 120
wait 1700
key w9 up
wait 200
expect echo_ret_db >-30
expect spring_ret_db >-40
# a plain stop: the returns ring on (the reference recording: their own steps, no cut)
wav stop2-ring.wav
tap PLAY
wait 600
said STOP
expect eng.play 0
expect echo_ret_db >-40
wavstop
#wav stop2-ring.wav peak<-1 click<-30
# a start 400 ms or more after the stop: normal (and the returns still there)
tap PLAY
wait 100
said PLAY
expect eng.play 1
wait 1000
key w9 down 120
wait 1700
key w9 up
wait 200
# stop twice: the second tap 150 ms after the stop
wav stop2.wav
tap PLAY
wait 70
expect echo_ret_db >-40
tap PLAY
wait 40
said TAILS OFF
expect eng.play 0
expect echo_ret_db -120
expect spring_ret_db -120
wait 300
wavstop
#wav stop2.wav peak<-1 click<-55 tail<-90
expect echo_ret_db -120
expect spring_ret_db -120
# a later start is normal: the transport runs and the echo works again
wait 600
tap PLAY
wait 100
said PLAY
expect eng.play 1
key w9 down 120
wait 600
key w9 up
wait 200
expect echo_ret_db >-60
tap PLAY
wait 50
param dev.tones 0
