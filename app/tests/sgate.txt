# The snare-keyed spring gate (P2.5; C13): each snare opens the spring's return for HOLD (default 133 ms), then it
# closes over RELEASE: a spring wash chopped into a tail on every snare. Here channel 1's tone is latched into the
# spring as a throw (pre-fader: its fader down, so only the spring is heard), soloed; ONE DROP's rim (beat 3,
# 1.6 s into each 3.2 s bar at 75 BPM) keys the gate even unheard. (A spring on a steady tone moves some on its
# own, ~15 dB: its resonances beating; gated it moves 40 dB and more.)
wait 600
param ch5.spring 0
param ch7.echo 0
param ch8.echo 0
param dev.tones 1
param ch1.level 0
param ch1.dest 1
param ch1.throw 1
param ch1.solo 1
wait 1500
tap PLAY
wav sgate-off.wav
wait 3200
wavstop
param sgate 1
wav sgate-on.wav
wait 3200
wavstop
# mid-bar, the rim long gone: the gate shut
wait 1000
expect spring_ret_db <-80
# just after the rim (bar 4, 1.6 s in at 9.6 + 1.6 = 11.2 s from PLAY): open
wait 3850
expect spring_ret_db >-40
tap PLAY
tap FX
tap FX
tap FX
tap FX
tap FX
tap FX
tap FX
wait 30
expect page 4
