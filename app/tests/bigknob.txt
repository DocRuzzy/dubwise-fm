# The Big Knob (P1.1; C3): a stepped 3rd-order Butterworth high-pass, 18 dB/oct: |H| = r^3 / sqrt(1 + r^6), r the
# frequency over the cutoff. Channel 1's test tone is 110 Hz at -20 dBFS: through 70 Hz -0.3 dB, through 100 Hz
# -1.9 dB, through 500 Hz -39.5 dB. SELECT turns it, a detent a step, at once.
wait 600
listen
param dev.tones 1
param ch1.solo 1
wait 50
expect out_db >-21
expect out_db <-19
turn SELECT 1
wait 50

wait 50

expect out_db >-62
expect out_db <-57
