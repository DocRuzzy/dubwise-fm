# Dice (roadmap 5.6, spec 4.10; app/dice.c): the SOUND view's last bank. ENV fires ROLL (a new sound inside the
# channel's bounds) or MUTATE (AMOUNT % of each range away); ENV held + a knob holds that field; KNOB 4 steps back
# through the channel's last 8 results; HOME + EDIT fires anywhere. Only the focused voice changes: never levels,
# sends, the bus or other channels. A fixed seed gives the same rolls. One undo step a fire. SETUP's DICE ON LINE
# makes a fire wait for the quantise line.
