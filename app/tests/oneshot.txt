# The one-shots (P2.6; C9, C10) on channel 8: black 8 HEY, black 9 HORN, black 10 LASER. Each plays to its end
# from a tap; velocity sets the level. Channel 8 sends to the echo, so a one-shot repeats like a thrown word (the return, after the shot has ended).
wait 600
listen
wav oneshot-hey.wav
tapkey b7
wait 50
said ONE SHOT HEY
expect ch8_db >-40
wait 650
expect echo_ret_db >-50
wait 3800
wavstop
wav oneshot-horn.wav
tapkey b8
wait 40
said ONE SHOT HORN
expect ch8_db >-40
wait 1500
wavstop
wav oneshot-laser.wav
tapkey b9
wait 40
said ONE SHOT LASER
wait 1200
expect ch8_db <-80
wavstop
