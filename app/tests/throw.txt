# Throws (P0.2): white keys 9-16 throw channels 1-8 to their destination, in full, pre-fader and pre-mute; a
# throw into the echo lifts its loop (halfway to unity, capped at 0.95) and brings in the 2T head. Destinations by
# default as spec 3: kick, hats, bass, lead, vox to the echo; snare to both; skank and organ to the spring.
wait 600
listen
param dev.tones 1
param ch5.spring 0
param ch8.echo 0
param ch7.echo 0
param ch1.mute 1
wait 50
expect eng.ch3.dest 0
expect eng.ch2.dest 2
expect eng.ch6.dest 1
expect echo_db -120
expect echo_g <200
# held: a muted channel thrown into the echo
key w8 down
wait 50
said THROW KICK
expect eng.ch1.throw 1
expect echo_db >-22
expect spring_db -120
expect echo_g >500
expect echo_g <951
key w8 up
wait 50
expect eng.ch1.throw 0
expect echo_db -120
expect echo_g <200
# pre-fader: the fader down does not matter (a pause first: a press within 300 ms of a tap would latch)
param ch1.level 0
wait 400
key w8 down
wait 50
expect echo_db >-22
key w8 up
wait 50
expect eng.ch1.throw 0
param ch1.level 100
param ch1.mute 0
# a double tap latches (snare: both buses); a tap lets it go
tapkey w9
wait 100
tapkey w9
wait 50
said THROW LATCHED SNARE
expect eng.ch2.throw 1
expect echo_db >-22
expect spring_db >-22
wait 600
expect eng.ch2.throw 1
tapkey w9
wait 50
said THROW OFF SNARE
expect eng.ch2.throw 0
