# The tape dive (P1.4; C4 drill 3): black 4 held glides the echo's time to DEPTH x T (default x1.5, clamped to what
# the tape holds: 8960 line samples) over DIVE TIME (default ~1 s), bending the repeats down; let go, it glides back.
wait 600
listen
param dev.tones 1
param ch1.solo 1
param ch1.echo 127
param echo.fb 80
wait 2000
expect echo_t 6614
wav dive.wav
key b3 down
wait 50
said TAPE DIVE
expect ui.echo.dive 1
wait 1500
expect echo_t >8800
key b3 up
wait 1600
expect echo_t <6700
wavstop
# FX: the TAPE bank's depth and glide
tap FX
tap FX
tap FX
tap FX
wait 30
said FX TAPE
turn K1 -64
wait 30
expect ui.dive.depth 0
said DIVE x1.00
