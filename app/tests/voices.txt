# The core voices (P0.5): MIDI channel 10 plays the drums by General MIDI note (36 kick, 37 rim, 42 / 46 closed /
# open hat), any other channel the bass. Each is recorded alone and checked: level, DC, the low end mono, the pitch
# (zero crossings), and how much of it is highs.
wait 600
# kick: a sine swept down to TUNE (default 42 -> 40 + 30 x 42/127 = 49.9 Hz)
wav kick.wav
midi 99 24 7F
wait 600
wavstop
# rim: inharmonic FM and a noise burst, short
wav rim.wav
midi 99 25 64
wait 400
wavstop
# hats: closed, then open; a closed hit chokes the open one
wav hat-closed.wav
midi 99 2A 64
wait 300
wavstop
midi 99 2E 64
wait 120
expect ch3_db >-50
wait 200
expect ch3_db >-80
midi 99 2E 64
wait 120
midi 99 2A 64
wait 200
expect ch3_db -120
# bass: A1 (55 Hz), held, then let go: it rings out in 60 ms
wav bass.wav
midi 90 21 64
wait 500
expect note 33
midi 80 21 00
wait 100
expect note -1
wavstop
wait 200
expect ch4_db -120
# all of them at once, and their cost
midi 99 24 7F
midi 99 25 64
midi 99 2E 64
midi 90 21 64
wait 40
perf
midi 80 21 00
