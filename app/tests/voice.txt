# The deejay voice (roadmap 6.6; app/engine/lpc.c, after SPELLBOX's speech chip): VOX's SAY picks a shout, and black 8
# says it in place of BIM (its name said on the screen); SAY PITCH raises it, STUTTER repeats frames, CRUSH drops the
# chip's bits; each shout a second or so, clean, and silent after. SAY 0: BIM again.
wait 600
listen
param ch8.echo 0
param v8.say 1
wait 30
wav say-selecta.wav
tapkey b7
wait 30
said ONE SHOT SELECTA
wait 1500
wavstop
param v8.say 2
wait 30
wav say-wheelup.wav
tapkey b7
wait 30
said ONE SHOT WHEEL UP
wait 1500
wavstop
param v8.say 3
param v8.say_pitch 7
param v8.stutter 60
param v8.crush 2
wait 30
wav say-bigup.wav
tapkey b7
wait 30
said ONE SHOT BIG UP
wait 2000
wavstop
param v8.say 0
wait 30
tapkey b7
wait 30
said ONE SHOT BIM
wait 600
